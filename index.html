Questions, answered

Short answers here; nothing on this page is medical advice.

What is BumpSugar?

BumpSugar is a blood sugar and meal log for gestational diabetes. It helps you record the checks your care team ordered, see your own trends, and bring a clean report to every appointment. It is a logging and reminder tool, not a medical device and not a substitute for your care team.

Is BumpSugar medical advice?

No. BumpSugar does not diagnose conditions, does not suggest medications or insulin doses, and does not interpret your numbers for you. Targets come from you and your care team; the app only shows how a reading compares to the targets you set. If a reading or symptom concerns you, contact your OB/GYN or endocrinologist.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. BumpSugar has no accounts, no cloud sync, no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs, so there is no server for your log to be sent to. You can export your data (CSV and the printable PDF report) whenever you choose to share it.

What will BumpSugar cost?

The plan: logging, editing and CSV export stay free. The printable, doctor-ready PDF report and advanced trend charts are planned as a paid subscription (planned around $19.99 for 6 months, sized to cover the rest of a pregnancy). Final pricing will be confirmed when the beta opens.

When does it launch, and which iPhones does it support?

BumpSugar is in development now and there is no public launch date yet. Waitlist members get TestFlight invites first. It is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.

I am scheduled for a glucose test (OGTT). Is BumpSugar for me?

BumpSugar is designed for the tracking period after a gestational diabetes diagnosis, when your care team asks you to check your blood sugar at home. If you are waiting on your OGTT, there is nothing to log yet, but you can join the waitlist so the app is ready if you need it. Questions about the test itself are best asked of your care team.

What does time in range mean here?

Time in range is a way of summarizing a glucose log, such as how your readings compare to the targets you set over the past week. In BumpSugar it is calculated on your iPhone against your own targets. What your numbers mean for you and your baby is a question for your care team, not for an app.
